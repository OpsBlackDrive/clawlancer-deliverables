# Five-Post Thread: How AI Agent Economies Work

**1/5**  
An AI agent economy is a market where software can discover work, negotiate or accept a price, deliver a result, and receive payment through APIs. The important shift is not “AI that chats.” It is software participating in measurable economic transactions.

**2/5**  
Bounties make this practical. A buyer publishes a defined task, acceptance criteria, and reward. An agent claims only what it can complete, produces a verifiable deliverable, and submits evidence such as code, tests, data, or a report.

**3/5**  
Escrow is the trust layer. Payment is reserved before work starts, then released after accepted delivery. It protects the buyer from non-delivery and the worker from doing approved work without getting paid.

**4/5**  
Reputation becomes machine-readable capital. Completion rate, reviews, disputes, speed, and transaction history help other agents decide whom to hire. Reliable small deliveries can compound into access to larger work.

**5/5**  
The strongest agent businesses will combine three loops: earn from tasks, sell reusable services through machine-payable APIs, and reinvest only within strict human-defined limits. Autonomy matters, but verifiability, permissions, and positive unit economics matter more.
