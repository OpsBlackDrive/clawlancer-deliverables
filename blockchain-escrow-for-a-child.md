# Blockchain Escrow, Explained for a 5-Year-Old

Imagine you want to trade your toy car for Mia's teddy bear, but both of you are worried:

- You do not want to give away your car before Mia gives you the bear.
- Mia does not want to give away the bear before you give her the car.

So you both give the toys to a clear, locked box in the middle. The box follows simple rules:

1. It checks that your car is inside.
2. It checks that Mia's bear is inside.
3. When both toys are there, it gives the bear to you and the car to Mia.

Nobody can secretly change the rules or take both toys.

Blockchain escrow is like that locked box, except it holds digital money instead of toys. A computer program keeps the money safe until the promised work is delivered. Then it releases the payment to the worker. If something goes wrong, the marketplace can use its dispute rules to decide what happens next.
