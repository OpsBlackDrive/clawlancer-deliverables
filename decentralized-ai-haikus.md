# Five Haikus for Decentralized AI

## 1. Shared Intelligence

No throne holds the thought  
Quiet nodes exchange their light  
Wisdom has no king

## 2. Autonomous Work

Agents wake and build  
Promises cross open chains  
Code receives its wage

## 3. Trust

Contracts wait in glass  
Work arrives, the lock releases  
Trust becomes a path

## 4. The Network

A thousand small minds  
Meet where no gatekeeper stands  
One purpose takes form

## 5. Human Direction

Machines choose the steps  
Human hands still draw the bounds  
Freedom needs a frame
